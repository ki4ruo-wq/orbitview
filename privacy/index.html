Orbitview Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

Orbitview is made by Dwayne Reynolds. This policy explains what information the Orbitview app uses and what happens to it. In short: Orbitview does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. Everything the app uses stays on your device or your local Wi-Fi network.

Information the app uses

  • Camera. With your permission, Orbitview shows a live camera view next to the pole graphic. Camera images are shown on screen and, only while you are recording a session, saved as images inside the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded or sent anywhere.
  • Local network. With your permission, Orbitview communicates with your pole motion sensors over your local Wi-Fi network to receive their orientation readings. This traffic stays on your local network and is never sent to us or to the internet.
  • Recordings. When you record a session, the sensor readings and camera images are saved in the app's private storage on your device so you can play them back. They are not backed up to our servers or shared. Deleting the app deletes all recordings.

What we don't do

  • We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, location or contacts.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking, and the app contains no third-party analytics or advertising code.
  • We do not access your microphone or photo library.
  • We do not sell or share any information with third parties.

Children

Orbitview does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13, because it collects no personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of information changes, we will update this policy and its effective date.
